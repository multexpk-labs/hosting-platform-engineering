# Billing & Entitlements

Billing determines commercial state; provisioning determines infrastructure state.

Do not assume they are identical.

## Example

`Invoice Paid → Entitlement Granted → Provisioning Request → Service Active`

A service may also be manually provisioned, prepaid, suspended, refunded, or awaiting verification.

## Important Concepts

- Product catalog
- Pricing
- Recurring billing
- Usage/hourly billing
- Credits
- Taxes where applicable
- Invoice state
- Service entitlement
- Grace periods
- Suspension
- Cancellation

Keep monetary calculations deterministic and auditable.
